The House That Kept Its Windows Shut

The cart track ended where the pines closed over it, and after that there was only the sound of water moving somewhere below the road. Arenheit counted the turns out of habit. Four, then the ruined gate, then the long wall with its ivy grown thick enough to hide a man.

Nobody in the village would say the family name aloud, which he took as a courtesy rather than a warning. Courtesies were cheaper here than bread.

He had learned in his first month that a house tells you what it wants before anyone in it does. This one wanted the shutters left as they were.

So he left them, and went in, and set his bag down on a floor that had been swept very recently by someone who had not wanted to be seen sweeping.

RULES
  • Title in Literata 600, flush left, ragged right, never centred.
  • ≤18 chars → 96px · ≤44 → 72px · ≤90 → 54px · beyond that truncate at a word with an ellipsis.
  • Gradient rule is the only ornament; it bleeds off the top edge.
  • Dark ground #12151B by default; light variant for e-ink devices.
