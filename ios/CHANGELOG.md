## 2.0 (2)

Everything the Android app can do, now on iPhone and iPad.

- Home is a summary of your categories, with new chapters underneath and your stats. Turn Continue, Favourites, Want to read and Reading & listening back on in Settings › Home, and choose the tab Novarr opens on.
- Categories: make, rename, reorder and hide them from Home. Your Library has a tab for each category, plus Uncategorised.
- Press and hold a series, then choose Select to pick several at once. You can add them to categories, mark them read or unread (with Undo), or download them for offline reading.
- Favourites and Want to read: tap the heart or the bookmark on any series.
- Comics and graphic novels have their own tab in your Library. They open in the page reader, left to right, and download for offline reading like manga.
- Books from your library open in the reader and show "The end" when you finish.
- Finishing a chapter, or tapping Next chapter, now always counts it as read, so your progress and stats are right.
- Listen to audiobooks in the new Listen tab. It has a full player with chapters, speed, skip back and forward, bookmarks with notes and a sleep timer.
- Audiobooks keep playing when your iPhone is locked. You can control them from the Lock Screen, Control Centre, headphones and car Bluetooth.
- Download audiobooks to listen offline. Novarr remembers where you stopped, on every device.
- Upload an audiobook from your iPhone, from a book's page, the Listen tab, or by opening audio files in Novarr from Files. Uploads pick up where they left off.
- Requests: ask for books, web novels, manga and comics. Type a title or an ISBN, or paste a Goodreads or Amazon link.
- See what others asked for and tap Me too, or browse the Discover shelves.
- Hold a book in Discover to request it straight into a category.
- Your requests and uploads are listed in My requests.
- Send a book to your Kindle or your email from its page, or ask for its audiobook.
- Send ePubs to Novarr: open an ePub in Novarr from Files or any app's Share sheet, or pick one in Settings.
- Your stats: streaks, chapters and listening time, a year of activity, top series and genres, and milestones. Find them on Home and in Settings.
- Notifications when a request is approved or available, and one combined notification for new chapters. iOS decides when Novarr checks in the background, so these can arrive a little later than on Android.
