# Novarr for Android: what's new

Newest first. Each section is `## <versionName> (<versionCode>)` from
`app/build.gradle.kts`, then a short bullet list in plain English for
readers. `make release-android` sends the section of the version it builds
with the APK (the update prompt in the app, the download page and the
"new version" email show it) and refuses to release while the section is
missing or still says "Notes to come".

## 1.18 (19)

- Categories: hold any book, web novel, manga or audiobook to select it, then add it to your categories or mark it read or unread in one go.
- Your Library has a tab for each category, plus Uncategorised.
- Hold a book in Browse to request it straight into a category; it greys out once it's taken.
- Home is now a summary of your categories, with new chapters underneath. Turn Continue, Favourites and Want to read back on in Settings › Home if you miss them.
- Manage categories: create, rename, reorder and hide them.
- Your stats: streaks, chapters and hours, a year of activity and more. Find them on Home and in Settings.
- Finishing a chapter, or tapping Next chapter, now always counts it as read, so your progress and stats are right.
- The update prompt shows what's new.

## 1.17 (18)

- Released as part of 1.18.

## 1.16 (17)

- Listen to audiobooks in the car with Android Auto.
- Home now starts with your own things: Continue, Favourites and your lists.
- Mark any series as a favourite to find it quickly.
- A more compact Continue row, so more fits on the screen.

## 1.15 (16)

- Upload an audiobook from your phone, from a book's page, the Listen tab or by sharing audio files to Novarr.
- Uploads carry on in the background and pick up where they left off after a dropped connection.
- Your uploads are listed in My requests.

## 1.14 (15)

- Books in Requests: search for a book and ask for it.
- A Discover shelf with books you might like.
- Send a book to your Kindle or your email from the app.
- Ask for the audiobook of a book that has none yet.

## 1.13 (14)

- Listen to audiobooks in the app, with lock-screen and headphone controls.
- Sleep timer, playback speed, skip silence and bookmarks with notes.
- Download audiobooks to listen offline.

## 1.12 (13)

- Want to read: save series to read next, from any series page.
- A Want to read row on Home.

## 1.11 (12)

- Share to Novarr: send ePub files from any app to add them to the library.

## 1.10 (11)

- A Books shelf and section for the books in the library.
